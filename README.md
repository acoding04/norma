# Norma

Open-source platform for managing AI projects with integrated EU AI Act compliance tracking.

## What it does

- **Continuous compliance monitoring** for EU AI Act and internal company guidelines (values, sustainability, ethics)
- **AI risk classification** (unacceptable, high, limited, minimal) per EU AI Act
- **Project management** with task tracking synced from GitHub Projects
- **Centralized documentation** organized by compliance framework
- **LLM-powered analysis** that maps project decisions against regulatory obligations and generates action plans
- **Norma chatbot** — contextual AI assistant for regulatory and project questions
